Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-25 · Last updated 2026-08-25

Orders aren't open yet. This describes what we collect today, and what we'll collect when they are.

Who's handling it

Talent Intelligence Strategies Global Inc. (泰然策略解密股份有限公司), UBN 60331107, No. 4-8, Qingdao E. Rd., Zhongzheng Dist., Taipei City 100, Taiwan.

What we collect

When you contact us or subscribe. Your name, job title, email, phone, organisation, what you're asking about and your message. Along with those we record when you submitted, the page you submitted from, your language setting and your browser's user agent string.

When orders open, and you order a report. All of the above, plus whatever you send us to evaluate:

  • a patent number, or
  • a PDF of a pending draft or specification, or
  • a description of an idea you haven't filed yet,

together with the report you picked, any notes you add and your contact email. This will be the most sensitive material we hold. An unfiled idea or an unpublished draft can lose its novelty if it gets out, so we treat it as confidential by default.

Payment. Card details will go to our payment provider and never touch TIS servers. We'll keep the result of the transaction and a payment reference.

Why we've got it

  • To produce and deliver the report you ordered.
  • To send your confirmation, your registration code and your completion notice.
  • To answer whatever you've asked us.
  • To send you what you subscribed to. Every newsletter will carry an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep our accounting, invoicing and other legal obligations straight.

Who else sees it

Producing a report will mean your material gets handled outside TIS. Worth knowing before you send us anything:

  • TIS staff in Taiwan. Reports will be produced by analysts leveraging closed-database AI tools, not by an unattended pipeline. Named people will read what you send, under confidentiality obligations, and only the ones who need to.
  • Innovue, for patent search and comparison against their database.
  • Our payment provider, for the card transaction.
  • Google, whose Sheets service already receives every contact and newsletter submission. That one is happening today; the three above start when orders do.

Which means your material crosses borders. We don't sell your data, and we don't let it be used to train AI models.

Separately from any of that, visiting the site at all involves two more parties. GitHub Pages hosts it, so it sees the IP address and request details of every visit. And if a page's local copy of a graphics or animation library fails to load, the page falls back to a public CDN — esm.sh, jsDelivr or unpkg — which then sees your IP as well. Neither of them gets anything you typed.

How long we keep it

What you send us to evaluate — the draft, the specification, the description of an unfiled idea — we keep only for as long as producing and supporting your report needs it. Ask us to delete it and we will, any time.

The finished report we'll keep for as long as we offer retrieval, because the Terms say you can come back for it whenever with your email and registration code.

Contact and enquiry records stay while we're dealing with you. Transaction records stay as long as accounting and tax law needs them. Newsletter subscriptions stay until you unsubscribe.

How we look after it

  • The site runs on HTTPS throughout.
  • Only staff who need access have it, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Every downloaded report will be watermarked with the account and the download time, so a leaked copy can be traced back.

What you can ask us to do

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act you can ask to see what we hold, get a copy, correct it, make us stop using it, or have it deleted. Email contact@tisglobalinc.com. We answer within the periods the Act sets — 15 days for access and copies, 30 days for corrections and deletion — and we'll tell you if we need one of the extensions it allows. It costs nothing and it never counts against you.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies at all — not for advertising, not for analytics, none. There is no analytics or advertising code on it either.

It does keep three things in your browser's own storage, and nowhere else: your language choice, so it sticks between visits; that you've dismissed the notice bar, for the rest of that browsing session; and that you've already seen the newsletter prompt, so it stays away for 30 days. None of it leaves your browser.

Changes

We update this as the service changes, and move the date at the top when we do.
