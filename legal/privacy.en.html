1. Who handles your data

Talent Intelligence Strategies Global Inc. (泰然策略解密股份有限公司), Unified Business Number 60331107, No. 4-8, Qingdao E. Rd., Zhongzheng Dist., Taipei City 100, Taiwan.

2. What we collect

When you contact us or subscribe. Name, job title, email, phone, organisation, your enquiry type and message. Alongside these we record the time of submission, the page you submitted from, your language setting and your browser's user agent string.

When you order a report. The above, plus what you submit for evaluation:

  • a patent number, or
  • a PDF of a pending draft or specification, or
  • a free-text description of an idea that has not been filed,

together with the report you chose, any notes you add, and your contact email. We treat this as the most sensitive material we hold. An unfiled idea or an unpublished draft can lose its novelty if it is disclosed, so it is handled as confidential by default rather than on request.

Payment. Card details are handled by our payment provider and never reach TIS servers. We keep the transaction result and a payment reference.

3. Why we hold it

  • To produce and deliver the report you ordered.
  • To send your confirmation, your registration code and your completion notice.
  • To answer enquiries you send us.
  • To send you material you subscribed to. Every newsletter carries an unsubscribe.
  • To meet accounting, invoicing and other legal obligations.

4. Who else sees it, and where it goes

Producing a report means your material is processed outside TIS. It is important that you know this before you submit anything:

  • TIS staff in Taiwan. Reports are produced by analysts working with AI, not by an unattended pipeline. Named staff read what you submit, under confidentiality obligations, limited to those who need it.
  • Innovue, for patent search and comparison against their database.
  • An AI language-model provider, for drafting assistance.
  • Our payment provider, for the card transaction.
  • Google, whose Sheets service receives contact and newsletter submissions.

This means your material crosses borders. We do not sell your data, and we do not allow it to be used to train AI models.

5. How long we keep it

Retention periods have not yet been set. We are not going to state a number we have not decided. Until they are fixed, we keep material only as long as needed to deliver your report, to let you retrieve it later, and to meet our legal obligations. If you want your submitted material deleted sooner, ask us and we will do it.

6. How it is protected

  • The site is served over HTTPS throughout.
  • Uploaded material is stored encrypted.
  • Access is limited to staff who need it, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Every downloaded report is watermarked with the account and download time, so a leaked copy can be traced.

7. Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act you may ask us to show you the data we hold, give you a copy, correct it, stop using it, or delete it. Write to contact@tisglobalinc.com and we will respond. Exercising these rights is free and never disadvantages you.

8. Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It stores your theme and language choices in your browser so they persist between visits, and records that you have dismissed a notice so it does not reappear. That information stays in your browser.

9. Changes

We update this policy as the service changes. The effective date at the top moves when we do.
