Who's handling it

Talent Intelligence Strategies Global Inc. (泰然策略解密股份有限公司), UBN 60331107, No. 4-8, Qingdao E. Rd., Zhongzheng Dist., Taipei City 100, Taiwan.

What we collect

When you contact us or subscribe. Your name, job title, email, phone, organisation, what you're asking about and your message. Along with those we record when you submitted, the page you submitted from, your language setting and your browser's user agent string.

When you order a report. All of the above, plus whatever you send us to evaluate:

  • a patent number, or
  • a PDF of a pending draft or specification, or
  • a description of an idea you haven't filed yet,

together with the report you picked, any notes you add and your contact email. This is the most sensitive material we hold. An unfiled idea or an unpublished draft can lose its novelty if it gets out, so we treat it as confidential by default — you don't have to ask.

Payment. Card details go to our payment provider and never touch TIS servers. We keep the result of the transaction and a payment reference.

Why we've got it

  • To produce and deliver the report you ordered.
  • To send your confirmation, your registration code and your completion notice.
  • To answer whatever you've asked us.
  • To send you what you subscribed to. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe on it.
  • To keep our accounting, invoicing and other legal obligations straight.

Who else sees it

Producing a report means your material gets handled outside TIS. Worth knowing before you send us anything:

  • TIS staff in Taiwan. Reports are produced by analysts working with AI, not by an unattended pipeline. Named people read what you send, under confidentiality obligations, and only the ones who need to.
  • Innovue, for patent search and comparison against their database.
  • An AI provider, for drafting help.
  • Our payment provider, for the card transaction.
  • Google, whose Sheets service receives contact and newsletter submissions.

Which means your material crosses borders. We don't sell your data, and we don't let it be used to train AI models.

How long we keep it

What you send us to evaluate — the draft, the specification, the description of an unfiled idea — gets deleted 24 months after your report is delivered. Ask us to delete it sooner and we will, any time.

The finished report we keep for as long as we offer retrieval, because the Terms say you can come back for it whenever with your email and registration code.

Contact and enquiry records stay while we're dealing with you. Transaction records stay as long as accounting and tax law needs them. Newsletter subscriptions stay until you unsubscribe.

How we look after it

  • The site runs on HTTPS throughout.
  • Anything you upload is stored encrypted.
  • Only staff who need access have it, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Every downloaded report is watermarked with the account and the download time, so a leaked copy can be traced back.

What you can ask us to do

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act you can ask to see what we hold, get a copy, correct it, make us stop using it, or have it deleted. Email contact@tisglobalinc.com. We answer within the periods the Act sets — 15 days for access and copies, 30 days for corrections and deletion — and we'll tell you if we need one of the extensions it allows. It costs nothing and it never counts against you.

Cookies

No advertising cookies, no analytics cookies. The site remembers your theme and language so they stick between visits, and remembers that you've dismissed a notice so it doesn't come back. That all stays in your browser.
