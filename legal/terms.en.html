2. What the service is

TIS produces patent evaluation reports in three forms — Snapshot, Study and Survey. Each is produced by TIS analysts working with AI, using patent data from Innovue's 180M-patent database. Reports are analytical documents. They are not legal opinions, not infringement analyses, and not investment advice. Section 7 sets out the limits in full.

3. Ordering and payment

Each report is priced individually and paid in two stages by card:

  • 10% deposit, charged when you submit your order. This starts production and issues your registration code.
  • 90% balance, charged before you download the finished report.

Between the two, you can preview the report's outline — the full scope of what each section covers — at no charge. The balance is not taken unless you choose to proceed.

Prices shown on the site are the prices that apply at the time you order. TIS may change prices for future orders; a change never affects an order already placed.

4. Refunds and cancellation

Invalid input, before any payment
No charge. Format errors and unfindable records are caught before the deposit is taken.
TIS cannot produce your report
The deposit is refunded in full. If we fail to deliver, you do not pay for the attempt. We notify you by email.
Report finished, balance unpaid
You are charged the 10% deposit only. You keep access to the outline preview; the full report stays locked. Nothing further is taken.
Re-downloading a report you have paid for
No further charge, ever. Your email and registration code retrieve it indefinitely.
A card payment fails
That stage does not proceed. Production does not start, or the download does not unlock. You can retry.
System failure on our side
No charge.
You change your mind after paying the deposit
Once production has begun, the deposit is not refundable. It pays for analyst time that has already been spent. If production has not yet begun, contact us and we will refund it.

A report is not legal, financial or investment advice. Decisions you take after reading one are yours.
