What a TIS rating is

Every patent TIS evaluates receives an SABCD grade and a score derived from 50 indicators. Of those, 34 are computed from structured data — citations, family size, legal status, claim structure and similar recorded facts. The remaining 16 are graded by natural-language processing of the patent text. Pillar weightings are documented in the published methodology.

Ratings are relative, not absolute

A grade and a percentile describe where a patent sits within a defined comparison pool. They are not absolute measures of quality, and they are not comparable across different pools. An S in one pool and an S in another do not mean the same thing. The composition of each pool is stated in the report that uses it.

Ratings move

Pools are re-ranked as new patents are granted and older ones lapse. A patent's rank, percentile and score can therefore change from one period to the next even when nothing about the patent itself has changed. A report is a snapshot at the date it was produced, not a standing statement.

Where the data comes from

Patent data is drawn from Innovue's 180M-patent database. That database is maintained carefully, but like every patent dataset it can carry publication delays, incomplete family records and transcription errors. TIS does not independently verify every underlying record.

Human and AI production

Reports are produced by TIS analysts working with AI, not by an unattended pipeline. AI assists with drafting and with the language-graded indicators; analysts direct the work and review the output. We say so plainly because it affects both what the report is worth and who has seen your material — see the Privacy Policy.

What a report is not

A TIS report is not a legal opinion. It is not a freedom-to-operate analysis, not an infringement or validity opinion, and not a substitute for review by a qualified patent attorney. It is not financial or investment advice.

Decisions you take after reading a report are yours. You should carry out your own due diligence and consult qualified professional advisers before acting.
