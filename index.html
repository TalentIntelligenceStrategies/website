Sustain

Local MRO capacity that keeps your contracts on track.

  • Repaired in Taiwan, ready when you need it

    Sites across the island, certified technicians and local spares mean repairs happen close to where your systems operate. Nothing ships back to the factory, so you skip the round-trip freight and export paperwork, and downtime is shorter. In Taiwan, high readiness is the reference that wins the next order.

  • Repairs without data leaks

    Your technical data is handled only by approved people, in approved spaces: segregated storage, logged access and visitor control, with an ISO 27001-certified capability partner. No parts of PRC origin or from red-supply-chain sources.

  • ITAR compliance, covered on our side

    Repairing defense articles requires a US-approved TAA, which you file. TIS prepares everything on the Taiwan side: personnel lists and screening, facility and data controls, contract annexes. That means fewer rounds of documents for you. Until the TAA is approved, we don't touch a single defense article.

Protect

Lock in your technology lead before competitors can move into the gap.

  • Know what Taiwan's buyers require

    We track Taiwan's military tenders for you, obtain and translate the tender documents, and set out the specification, localisation and supply-chain requirements, so your product plan and patent strategy are aimed at real demand from day one.

  • See the patent risk before you launch

    Before your systems arrive, we check local patents against a 180M-patent database, flag infringement risk, and propose a design-around or a licence.

  • A Taiwan-first patent strategy

    Once your systems are fielded in Taiwan, they can be studied and copied. We review your Taiwan patent coverage and close the gaps first, then plan for Japan, Korea, Singapore, Australia, India and Europe, with quarterly tracking of technology white space and competitor moves.

  • Checked at every step, from filing to office action

    From pre-filing strategy and draft-quality review to office-action strategy at 4 patent offices: Taiwan, the US, Japan and Korea. Our fee depends on whether your protection works, not on how many applications you file.

How the retainer works

License

Flexible licensing that speeds up local market entry and partnerships.

  • The right licensing partners, in and out

    Taiwan has no established patent-licensing intermediary. We find the local patents you need to license, from ITRI, NCSIST, universities and local makers, and the local suppliers, integrators and operators who need your technology. Then we help negotiate the terms.

  • Make your Taiwan patents pay

    The patents you file in Taiwan can be licensed to local companies, turning them into royalty income and leverage in negotiations.

  • Pay for results, carry less risk

    Licensing services can run on a success fee: you pay when a licence is signed, not a large advisory fee up front.

  • Faster local partnerships

    A licence lowers the technical and regulatory hurdles of working with local assemblers and suppliers.
