Sustainment for UV systems fielded in Taiwan

Maintenance, repair, spares, field service and warranty across Taiwan, through an island-wide service partner, under one contract.

Request a scoping conversationHow engagements start

Every week without a local sustainment partner is a week of lead-time risk, warranty exposure and readiness questions from your customer.

You have systems fielded in Taiwan, or an order that will put them there.

Building your own organisation takes a year you don't have.

Local MRO capacity that keeps your contracts on track.

  • Repaired in Taiwan, ready when you need it

    Sites across the island, certified technicians and local spares mean repairs happen close to where your systems operate. Nothing ships back to the factory, so you skip the round-trip freight and export paperwork, and downtime is shorter. In Taiwan, high readiness is the reference that wins the next order.

  • Repairs without data leaks

    Your technical data is handled only by approved people, in approved spaces: segregated storage, logged access and visitor control, with an ISO 27001-certified capability partner. No parts of PRC origin or from red-supply-chain sources.

  • ITAR compliance, covered on our side

    Repairing defense articles requires a US-approved TAA, which you file. TIS prepares everything on the Taiwan side: personnel lists and screening, facility and data controls, contract annexes. That means fewer rounds of documents for you. Until the TAA is approved, we don't touch a single defense article.

One package, from spares to paperwork

Ten services in four groups, run by TIS as one program with one SLA.

Maintenance and repair

Preventive and corrective maintenance to your OEM manuals, with failure analysis reported back.

Spares and logistics

Parts sourcing and demand planning, with customs handled by a licensed importer.

Field service and warranty

Island-wide field service, emergency response and warranty administration on your behalf.

Compliance and records

Supplier qualification, traceability and audit-ready records, with no red-supply-chain sources.

Service capacity

Island-wide today

Service capacity comes from Suntek Group, with PG Union Corp first: a nationwide dealer network, full-service workshops and group-wide parts logistics.

  • 30+years of local service operations in Taiwan
  • 9companies across Suntek Group's five business lines

How a service partner becomes UV-grade

FairTech, the contracted capability lifter, brings each facility up level by level. Until a site qualifies, deeper work goes to FairTech under the same SLA.

  1. L0

    Baseline

    Workshop, parts logistics, customs and island-wide dispatch.

  2. L1

    Line maintenance

    FairTech-certified technicians for inspection, servicing and part swaps.

  3. L2

    Component diagnostics

    Test benches, failure analysis and configuration control.

  4. L3

    Depot-level repair

    Avionics, electronics and composite repair and overhaul in-house.

Two lanes, one compliance program

We structure the engagement around your licence, not the other way round.

Lane A

Commercial and dual-use

Work starts once your licence or exemption is confirmed.

Lane B

Defense articles

Work starts only once your Technical Assistance Agreement (TAA) is in force.

Each engagement is assigned a lane at scoping. Classifications are confirmed per program.

Your platform, your fleet size, your lane: Phase 1 scopes all three in 6–10 weeks.
