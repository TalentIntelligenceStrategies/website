## Long form — full "How it works / Why it's precise" (if a deeper section is needed)

**Lead:** Keyword search finds patents that use your words. Survey finds patents that do what yours does.

**It reads meaning, not words.** Every patent — and your input — is turned into a high-dimensional representation of the *technology itself*: the problem it solves, the method it uses, the effect it achieves. Survey then measures true technical proximity, surfacing the ten closest even when they describe the same idea in completely different language — or come from an adjacent field you'd never have thought to search.

**It understands patents.** The engine is built on patent text — claims, specifications, the language inventions are actually written in — so it reads a filing the way an examiner would, not the way a search box does.

**It works from anything.** A granted number, a rough draft, or a one-paragraph idea. Because Survey matches on technology, not paperwork, a half-formed concept returns the same calibre of results as an issued patent. *(Behind the scenes, we first distil your input into a clean technical description — coherent meaning in, precise matches out.)*

**Then it tells you why.** The ten come back ranked by closeness, each with a plain-English read on the overlap — where the claims converge, the technical approach, the players involved — so a match score is never just a number you have to take on faith.

**Close:** Drawing on live, continuously updated patent data, Survey turns a question that used to take an analyst days — *what's already out there, and how close is it?* — into ten answers, and the reasons behind each one.
