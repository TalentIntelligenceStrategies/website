Signal cover directions

Three cover treatments for the Signal report, each across all three report tiers. Variant B is wired into the retrieval module today; A and C are built and waiting on a decision. Click any sheet to see it full size.

Sheet width
Condition

The retrieval screen shows the cover at 260 px behind a 4 px blur, locked until the balance is paid. A cover carries no grade, no radar and no pillar ladder, so the width of the first word — Snapshot, Study, Survey — is the only thing left that tells the tiers apart. Set the width to 260, turn the blur on, and judge them there first.

A

Ink on paper, rule-led

Alternative

Keeps the interior pages' dark masthead and colophon, so the cover reads as page one of one continuous document. The title sits in the upper middle and the order facts are pinned to the foot, with the page's slack split into two equal fields rather than collecting in one hole in the middle.

B

Centred classical

Wired in

Drops both bands and goes white to the edge, with the lockup set large and centred on the axis — the bound-report convention, where the cover is its own surface and the document proper starts behind it. Centring is the whole argument for this direction, so it is applied without exception; one left-aligned element would read as a mistake.

assets/product-shots/signal/signal-report-cover-variant-b.html · rendered to the covers the product page serves

C

Bottom-anchored

Alternative

Groups the whole lot — kicker, report type, subject, order facts — as one block at the foot, under a single open field. A cover whose title sits low reads as a monograph rather than a form, and grouping the metadata with the type gives the page one deliberate opening instead of content, hole, content. The bands are kept, so it bookends dark to dark like the interior pages.

What the cover replaced

The blurred thumbnail used to show the report's first interior page. Each tier carried different artwork there — a radar, a band of pillar bars, a ladder of the closest patents — and those shapes survived the blur, which is how the tiers read apart at 260 px. The covers give that up in exchange for looking like a document rather than a dashboard; the outline printed beside the thumbnail now carries the tier.
