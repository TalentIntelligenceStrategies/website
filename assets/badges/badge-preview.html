Standalone Seal Badges

The Seal · Standalone credential mark (per brand/components.md §Seal · Standalone), reorganised into four structural variants across the four issuers — TIS + partners NYCU · IPIC · III. Aesthetic (geometry, colours, type) is unchanged; only the seal's contents vary per row. Each badge is a self-contained <svg> — download it as a true SVG or a transparent @2x PNG.
Note: SVG/PNG export embeds Urbanist for faithful Latin text. Row-4 Chinese uses Noto Sans TC on-screen; standalone export falls back until the ZH strings are confirmed and the subset is embedded.

Row 1 · Full

Top-arc org line · center logo · bottom-arc credential count. Identical to the seal in components-preview.html.

Row 2 · No count

Bottom-arc count stripped. Top-arc org line + center logo retained.

Row 3 · Initials

Logo + count stripped → org initials in the center. TIS keeps its cube submark (ours is the exception).

Row 4 · Chinese

Logo + count stripped → Chinese organisation name in the center. Partner ZH strings are DRAFT — verify.

Wild card

Faceted Mint

An angular departure from the round LEED disc. The hexagon is the isometric silhouette of the TIS cube; faint spokes radiate to each vertex like a cut gem's facets — patents as the undervalued asset, made into a minted token. Typography is flat, not curved, and the identity is stripped to essentials: mark · initials · license no. Same four issuers, same palette.
