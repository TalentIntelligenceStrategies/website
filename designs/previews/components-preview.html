Battery cell array cooling system with non-uniform liquid channels for balanced flow resistance
Abstract

This invention discloses a liquid cooling apparatus for a battery cell array, comprising (a) a plurality of channels arranged between adjacent cells, (b) each channel having a non-uniform cross-section configured to balance flow resistance, and (c) a manifold coupled to the channels to distribute coolant. This method is suitable for high-density electric-vehicle battery packs operating at sustained discharge rates.
