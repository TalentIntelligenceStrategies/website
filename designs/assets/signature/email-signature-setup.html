Signature Set-up

Steps

  1. Below, find the card with your name and click Copy for Gmail. The button turns green to confirm.
  2. Open Gmail. Click the gear icon (top right) → See all settings.
  3. You're on the General tab. Scroll down to the Signature section.
  4. Click + Create new. Name it TIS (or whatever you like) → Create.
  5. Click into the empty signature editor on the right, then paste. Your signature should appear exactly as it looks in the preview.
  6. Under Signature defaults, set both FOR NEW EMAILS USE and ON REPLY/FORWARD USE to your new TIS signature.
  7. Just below, you'll see a checkbox: Insert signature before quoted text in replies, and remove the "--" line that precedes it. Leave it unchecked.
  8. Scroll to the bottom of the settings page and click Save Changes.
  9. Send a test email to yourself to confirm everything renders correctly.
    Heads up — the logo may flicker once on the first load as it's pulled from our hosted server. After that it's cached, so subsequent opens render instantly.
