Common questions

The questions you'd ask before signing.

Why a bundle instead of buying individual patents?

A bundle eliminates "cherry-picking paralysis" — you get vetted, cross-category coverage in one transaction. Per-patent cost drops 30–40% vs. one-off acquisition. And the goal isn't ownership; it's a legal shield. Non-exclusive licensing fits the use case exactly.

What if a patent in my bundle gets invalidated?

The platform runs a dynamic pool compensation mechanism. If a patent is revoked mid-contract, TIS replaces it with an equivalent-tier patent at no cost. Coverage gaps are on us.

How is non-exclusive licensing different from ownership?

Non-exclusive = you can't block others from using the same patents (other licensees can also access them), but the cost is about 1/20 of ownership. Non-exclusive fits defense; if you want offense (transfer / exclusive licensing / sale), that runs through a separate brokerage service.

Do renewals get a discount?

No — this is a May 2026 policy change. Renewal monthly rate = first-subscription MSRP × period discount, no further stacking. The reasoning: long-term patent value is preserved through renewal normalization (full MSRP every year), not through bulk-discount erosion.

Which jurisdictions are covered?

Four primary: US, EP, JP, TW. They reflect Taiwan's top export markets and tech-supply-chain strategic alignment. Need other jurisdictions? Sales can quote case-by-case.

Can I upgrade mid-contract?

Yes — upgrade to a higher tier or add a new jurisdiction. The difference is calculated per-patent-per-day (v8.1.0+). No renegotiation. Downgrades wait until term end.
