The PSS pipeline

  1. 50 traceable indicators

    Every pillar unpacks into 4–8 indicators: raw value, percentile, weight — traceable to the formula.

  2. 8 strength pillars

    Citation, legal, technical, market and four more — eight pillars, independently weighted.

  3. 0–100 objective score

    Raw values are normalised against the same-industry pool, then aggregated into a 0–100 PSS.

  4. S–D SABCD grade

    A tier is a discretisation of cohort percentile. S is the top 15% of the pool.

Reports are produced by TIS analysts working with AI, not an automated pipeline. That's a deliberate trade: we spend real working time to return precision and depth a black-box tool can't — while still moving far faster than the 1–4 weeks of a traditional consultant.
