The PSS pipeline

  1. 50 traceable indicators

    Every pillar unpacks into 4–8 indicators: raw value, percentile, weight, each traceable to the formula.

  2. 8 strength pillars

    Citation, legal, technical, market and four more, each independently weighted.

  3. 0–100 objective score

    Raw values are ranked inside the full 1,433-patent pool, one cross-industry benchmark, then aggregated into a 0–100 PSS.

  4. S–D SABCD grade

    A tier is a discretisation of pool percentile. S is the top 15% of the pool.
