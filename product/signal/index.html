Insights/Patent Strength Grading

Know what a patent is worth before you file, license or defend it.

Patent Strength Grading

Bring a granted patent, a pending draft, or just an idea. Get back a strength report you can trace line by line, or the ten patents that matter most.

Request a reportSee a sample report

Two reports.

One patent, two frames: the evidence behind its grade, and the field around it.

How the grade is calculated.

50 indicators, eight pillars, one 0–100 score, and a grade from S to D. Every step traces back to a number you can check.

Powered by

1433TIS and partner patents
in the reference pool

Your patent is ranked against 1433 patents held by TIS and its partners, which we also license. Not every patent in the world, and not an independent benchmark.

50indicators

What gets measured

Most come from the patent record: citations, family, claims, dates, legal status. The rest are graded by closed-database AI tools reading the text.

8pillars

Eight pillars, weighted apart

Indicators roll up into eight pillars, each weighted independently. Text Quality and Legal Strength carry eight indicators each; Network Centrality carries four.

0–100in-pool percentile

A raw value becomes a position

Every raw value becomes its percentile in the pool. 47 forward citations is scored on where 47 falls among 1433.

S–Dgrade

A position becomes a letter

The weighted score's percentile resolves to a grade. S is the top 15%, then A 20, B 30, C 20, D 15.
