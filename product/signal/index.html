FAQ

The Patent Strength Score (PSS) engine decomposes every patent into 50 quantitative signals — citation impact, legal strength, technical value, market coverage, claim & specification quality, network centrality, licensing potential, and forward-looking value. Eight pillars, each independently weighted. Transparency over precision.

Signal reads your patent's IPC class, technical domain, and filing window, then matches it to 100+ peers from the TIS pool — pre-scored on Innovue WEBPAT. The same pool feeds bundles on the TIS Licensing Platform. This isn't "percentile vs every patent on Earth." It's percentile vs the patents in your own field.

Yes. Click any pillar to see its 4–8 underlying indicators. Click any indicator to see the formula, the raw value, and the cohort median. No "our model says." Signal Briefs are informational — not legal or investment advice.

Brief (15 credits) covers the SABCD verdict, PSS magnitude, peer percentile, cohort rank, and eight-pillar radar. Pro (+15 credits) adds the 50-indicator summary scorecard, rating rationale, top 10 strengths with contribution percentages, top 10 risks with sensitivity analysis, cross-scenario stability matrix (licensing / litigation / M&A), licensing strategy with counter-party map, design-around guidance, full 50-indicator drilldown with formulas, and PDF + JSON export. +15 credits upgrades a Brief to Pro in under thirty seconds.
