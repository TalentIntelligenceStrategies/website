FAQ

The Patent Strength Score (PSS) engine decomposes every patent into 50 quantitative signals — citation impact, legal robustness, market coverage, licensing potential, forward technology, family breadth, maintenance vitality, competitive density. Eight pillars, each independently weighted. Transparency over precision.

Signal reads your patent's IPC class, technical domain, and filing window, then matches it to 100+ peers from the TIS pool — pre-scored on Innovue WEBPAT. The same pool feeds bundles on the TIS Licensing Platform. This isn't "percentile vs every patent on Earth." It's percentile vs the companies you'll actually compete with.
