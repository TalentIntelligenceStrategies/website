Common questions

The questions you'd ask first.

Why not just use Google Patents?

Free tools show you patent text, but not how the patent stacks against peers in your vertical, whether it's defensible in court, or whether it has licensing value. Signal adds the judgment layer VCs need — peer benchmarking + SABCD rigor — in 30 minutes.

How do you guarantee the SABCD grades are accurate?

Every grade runs through Innovue's PSS (Patent Strength Score) engine — the same 50-indicator, 8-pillar system that powers TIS Licensing Platform. Innovue's WEBPAT covers 170M patents across 100+ countries. Every number is traceable: click any SABCD grade, drill into the 50-item scorecard. Transparency > precision. Different scores from counsel usually mean a different peer pool (we use TIS's standardized pool; they may hand-pick 5–10 comparables).

What if the patent isn't in your database?

When you submit a patent, Innovue's API extracts its 50 attributes in 15–20 minutes. If it's new or obscure, it may not be in our standardized pool as a "peer," but it will be scored and ranked by percentile against peers matching its IPC classification. You still get SABCD grade, percentile, and radar chart — the peer universe just reflects what's available in the curated TIS pool.

How fast can it go?

The 30-minute Brief is a workflow choice, not a technical limit. Innovue extraction is 15–20 min; TIS scoring + email is 10 min. Why async? Because VCs batch-screen — Monday morning you see 8 decks, submit 8 patents, walk to meetings, return 30 minutes later to 8 Briefs in your inbox. Pro upgrades are instant (< 30 sec) because attributes are already extracted at Brief time.

Is Signal legal or investment advice?

No. Every Signal report carries a disclaimer: this is not legal, financial, business, or investment advice. It's a decision-support tool for analysts and IC members; high-stakes M&A and litigation still call for counsel. We name the boundary so the SABCD numbers you cite stand on solid ground.

Can my whole team share one subscription?

Yes. Monthly subscriptions are billed per company domain (e.g., @acme-ventures.com), not per seat. Up to 5 team members share one pooled wallet; every query draws from the same monthly bucket. No "buy 3 seats = 3× the price" trap.
